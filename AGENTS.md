# Article standard

Every substantive article on this site must answer four questions before it is published:

1. What is the original observation or argument?
2. What personal experience or credible evidence supports it?
3. What important counterargument might challenge it?
4. What should the reader understand or do differently?

When drafting, rating or editing an essay, check it against these first. If one is missing, flag it and propose a fix before calling the piece finished. Short notes and non-essay pages are exempt.
